Develop novel computational disease models that could be used to identify factors that impact the rapid/slow progression of Type 1 Diabetes ("T1D") in infants and young children who are genetically pre-disposed and may be pre-symptomatic clinically. Such methodologies and models may <br> <li> incorporate heterogeneous features coming from multiple sites and assessments covering multiple aspects of T1D. <li> leverage diverse data sets to accommodate noise and uncertainty in study data. <li> provide comprehensive view of risk factors that impact the onset of T1D in different time horizons. <br/> <b> Role </b> I lead the efforts of developing AI models for disease progression modeling, which lead to publications in high-impact journals such as Lancet D&E (IF 45), Lancet C&A (IF 38), Diabetes Care (IF 19), etc. <br/> <i> IBM Research.  May 2017 - May 2022 </i>.
